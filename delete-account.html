Delete your TripMate account

Last updated: October 6, 2026

You can ask us to delete your TripMate account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: open Settings, then tap Request account deletion. This opens an email to us with the subject filled in. Send it from the email address you signed up with.
  2. Or by email: write to saathi.app.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my TripMate account", from the email address you signed up with.

What happens next

  • We confirm the request came from the account owner.
  • Within 30 days of confirming, we delete your profile, photos, verification selfies and the activity linked to your account.
  • We may keep certain records where we need them for legal or safety reasons, for example a report made about a user.
  • Messages you already sent in a shared chat may stay visible to the other people in that chat.
  • Deleting your account cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with a new account.

See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
