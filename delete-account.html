Delete your TripMate account

Last updated: October 7, 2026

You can delete your TripMate account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. In the app: open Settings, tap Account & data, then Delete account, and follow the steps.
  2. Or by email: write to saathi.app.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my TripMate account", from the email address you signed up with. We will confirm it is you and then start the same 30-day process.

What happens next

  • Your account is deactivated straight away. Other travellers can no longer see your profile.
  • After 30 days, we permanently delete your profile, photos, verification selfies and the activity linked to your account, including the plans you created and the messages you sent. We may keep certain records only where the law requires it.
  • Changed your mind? Log back in any time within those 30 days and tap Keep my account. Everything is exactly as you left it.
  • Once the 30 days have passed, deletion cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with a new account.
  • Anything another person has already copied, such as a screenshot, is outside our control.

Want a break instead? You can pause your profile from the same screen, which hides you from other travellers without deleting anything.
