How it works

Three steps from "I want to go" to "we're going".

01

Post a plan

Pick the place, the dates and how many people can come. Open to all, or you approve who joins.

02

See who's going

Browse plans by destination and type of trip, check who is hosting and who has joined, then ask to join.

03

Chat, then go

Everyone on a trip gets a group chat, so plans and meeting points are sorted before anyone leaves home.

You're in control

You decide who can find you.

Meeting new people should feel comfortable. These controls are in the app from day one.

  • Choose who can see your profile
  • Selfie verification, with a badge on verified profiles
  • Hosts can approve who joins their trip
  • Report or block anyone in a couple of taps
  • Group chats only with the people on your trip
