Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Saathi ("we", "us") is offered by Wanderly Technologies. This policy explains what information we collect through the Saathi app, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account info: email address, and phone number if you complete phone verification.
  • Profile info: display name, date of birth, gender, home city/state, bio, interests, travel style, and languages you choose to add.
  • Photos: profile photos you upload, and a verification selfie used only to confirm it matches your profile photo.
  • Trip & social activity: plans you create or join, group chat messages, ratings and reviews you give or receive after a trip.
  • Device info: basic crash and error diagnostics, and a push-notification token if you enable notifications.

How we use it

  • To create and display your profile to other travellers.
  • To match you with relevant trips and travellers, and enable chat between people on the same trip.
  • To verify your identity via selfie comparison, so profiles on Saathi are more trustworthy.
  • To send you notifications about matches, messages, and trip updates (only if enabled).
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. Other Saathi users can see your public profile, photos, and anything you post in shared trip chats. We use the following service providers to run the app, each of which processes data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and our backend functions.
  • Expo — app infrastructure and push notification delivery.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove most profile information at any time from within the app.
  • You can block other users, which prevents them from contacting you.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us (below).

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile, photos, and messages within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records for legal or safety reasons.

Children

Saathi is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data — email us at saathi.app.dev@gmail.com.
